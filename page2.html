About IPL

The Indian Premier League (IPL) is a professional Twenty20 cricket league that features multiple franchise teams from different cities.


Founded in 2008, the league has grown into one of the most popular sporting events in the world. It combines entertainment, sportsmanship, and competitive cricket.


Each season consists of league matches followed by playoffs and a grand final.
